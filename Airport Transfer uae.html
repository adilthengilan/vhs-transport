Airport transfer, answered.

What is the best way to get an airport transfer in Abu Dhabi?

Book a pre-arranged transfer with a local operator such as VHS Passengers Transport. A driver is assigned to your flight, receives you on arrival and takes you directly to your destination.

How do I book an airport transfer?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp or email info@vhstransport.com with your flight number, date, pick-up point and number of passengers. Our team will confirm the vehicle and driver.

Is the airport transfer service available 24 hours?

Yes. VHS Passengers Transport operates 24/7, so early-morning departures and late-night arrivals can be arranged.

Which airport do you serve in Abu Dhabi?

We provide pick-up and drop-off for arrivals and departures at Zayed International Airport in Abu Dhabi.

Is there a meet and greet service on arrival?

Yes. A professional driver receives your passengers and helps them get settled in the vehicle.

Can you transfer groups, staff or families?

Yes. We handle transfers for individual passengers as well as groups travelling together, including company staff, guests and families.

How is the pick-up time decided?

We plan the pick-up around the flight details you share, so the driver is on time for departures and ready for arrivals.

What details do I need to share when booking?

Share your flight number, travel date, pick-up or drop-off address, number of passengers and luggage. This lets our team assign the right vehicle and plan the timing.

How far in advance should I book an airport transfer?

We recommend booking in advance so the right vehicle and driver are reserved. For same-day requests, call our office and we will confirm availability.

How much does an airport transfer in Abu Dhabi cost?

The rate depends on the vehicle type, the route and the number of passengers. Send us your details and our team will confirm the price before the trip.

Which areas do you cover?

We provide airport pick-up and drop-off across Abu Dhabi, including Al Jazira, the city centre, the islands and surrounding districts. For other locations in the UAE, contact our team to confirm.
