Luxury bus rental FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is luxury bus rental for travel?

It is a group transportation service where a bus and professional driver are booked for your route and schedule, such as a transfer, an airport trip or a journey with several stops. VHS Passengers Transport provides it in Abu Dhabi and across the UAE.

How do I book a luxury bus with VHS Transport?

Send us your route, travel dates and group size by phone on +971 2 440 3060, by email at info@vhstransport.com or through WhatsApp. Our team will plan the trip and confirm the details with you.

Where does VHS Transport operate?

VHS Passengers Transport is based in Abu Dhabi and provides group travel by bus across the UAE.

Are the vehicles tracked and the drivers trained?

Yes. All VHS vehicles use a GPS system that supports visibility and operational reporting, and drivers are trained in etiquette and rigorous safety standards.

Is support available around the clock?

Yes. VHS runs a 24/7 operations team that handles booking and support for every journey.

What details do I need for a quote?

Your pick-up and drop-off locations, travel dates and times, number of passengers and any extra stops. With these details the team can plan a safe, punctual and cost-effective trip.
