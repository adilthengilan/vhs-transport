01 / School & college transport

Getting students to class on time.

VHS Passengers Transport moves students between home and school or college every day, by bus and van, on routes planned around your timings.

Parents and institutions need to know their students will arrive on time, every day. We plan routes around school hours and the pick-up areas your students use, and we keep vehicles tracked by GPS for reporting.

Tell us about your campus, student numbers and pick-up areas, and we will propose a route plan. See our pages on school transportation in Abu Dhabi and school transportation in Al Jazira. For group trips and events, see our bus and car rental.

FAQ

School transportation, answered.

What is school transportation?

School transportation is the daily movement of students between home and school or college, usually by bus or van on planned routes and timings.

Do you provide school transport in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. VHS Passengers Transport is based in Abu Dhabi and provides school and college transport across Abu Dhabi, including Al Jazira. For other UAE locations, contact our team to confirm.

How do I request school or college transport?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp at +971 56 343 4546 or email info@vhstransport.com with your institution, student numbers, pick-up areas and school timings. Our team will propose a route plan and a quote.

Are the routes planned around school hours?

Yes. Routes and timings are planned around your school or college hours and the pick-up areas your students use.

Are your vehicles GPS tracked?

Yes. GPS on our vehicles supports visibility and reporting for institutions.

Are your drivers trained?

Our drivers are trained for passenger transport. Tell us your institution's safety requirements, and we will confirm the standards we can meet.

Can you transport students to and from colleges?

Yes. We provide transport for both schools and colleges, with routes adjusted to each institution's timings.

How much does school transportation cost?

The price depends on the number of students, the routes, the vehicles and the schedule. Send us your requirements and our team will confirm a quote.
