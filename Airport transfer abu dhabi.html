Airport transfer in Abu Dhabi. We take it from here.

FAQ

Airport transfer Abu Dhabi, answered.

Is there a 24-hour airport transfer in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. VHS Passengers Transport operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so early-morning departures and late-night arrivals can be arranged.

Which airport do you serve in Abu Dhabi?

We provide pick-up and drop-off at Zayed International Airport (AUH), formerly Abu Dhabi International Airport. For other UAE airports, contact our team to confirm.

How do I book an airport transfer?

Send your flight number, travel date, pick-up or drop-off address, number of passengers and luggage by phone at +971 2 440 3060, on WhatsApp at +971 56 343 4546, or by email at info@vhstransport.com. Our team confirms the vehicle, driver and price before the trip.

Is there a meet and greet service on arrival?

Yes. A professional driver meets your passengers at Zayed International Airport and helps them settle into the vehicle.

How is the pick-up time decided?

The pick-up is planned around the flight details you share, so the driver is on time for departures and ready for arrivals.

How much does an airport transfer in Abu Dhabi cost?

The rate depends on the vehicle type, the route and the number of passengers. Our team confirms the price before the trip.

Can you transfer groups, staff or families?

Yes. We transfer individual passengers as well as groups travelling together, including company staff, guests and families.

Which areas in Abu Dhabi do you cover?

We provide airport pick-up and drop-off across Abu Dhabi, including Al Jazira, the city centre, the islands and surrounding districts. For other UAE locations, contact our team to confirm.

How far in advance should I book?

Book in advance so the right vehicle and driver are reserved. For same-day requests, call our office and we will confirm availability.
