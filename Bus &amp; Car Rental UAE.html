What vehicles can I rent?

VHS Passengers Transport offers cars, vans and buses in a range of sizes, so you can match the vehicle to your group.

Do you offer short-term and long-term rental?

Yes. You can rent for a day, a season or longer, depending on what your business or group needs.

How do I get a bus or car rental quote?
