Abu Dhabi labour transport, answered.

Who provides staff bus service in Abu Dhabi?

VHS Passengers Transport provides staff and labour transport in Abu Dhabi by bus and van, scheduled around your shifts, with GPS on its vehicles.

How do I arrange labour transportation?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp or email info@vhstransport.com with your headcount, locations and shift timings. Our team will recommend the right mix of buses and vans and send a customised plan.

Can you schedule transport around our shifts?

Yes. Pick-ups and drop-offs are timed around your shift patterns and site requirements, so your teams reach work on time and return safely.

What vehicles do you use for workforce transport?

We use large-capacity buses and vans to move groups of workers together efficiently, chosen to suit your headcount.

Is labour transportation cost-effective?

We plan routes and vehicles to keep transport cost-effective, and our team offers a customised plan with competitive rates. Contact us with your requirements for a quote.

Do you operate 24/7?

Yes. VHS Passengers Transport operates 24/7, which supports early, late and night shift patterns.
