Abu Dhabi city tour, answered.

How do I arrange an Abu Dhabi city tour?

Contact VHS Passengers Transport with your group size, date and the places you want to visit. We will plan the route, vehicle and timings.

Do you offer half-day and full-day tours?

Yes. We arrange half-day, full-day or custom routes, planned around your group and your interests.

How do I plan a city tour?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp or email info@vhstransport.com with your group size, dates and the places you want to visit. Our team will plan the route, vehicle and timings.

Which places can be included in an Abu Dhabi city tour?

You can include places such as the Corniche, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Island and Saadiyat Island, as well as shopping and waterfronts. We plan the route around your choices.

What vehicles are used for the tours?

We use air-conditioned cars, vans and buses, chosen to match your group size.

Are the drivers experienced?

Yes. Our drivers are courteous, know the city and keep your day on schedule.

Who are the tours suitable for?

Tours suit families, corporate groups and delegations. We plan each one around the visitors and the time available.

How much does an Abu Dhabi city tour cost?

The price depends on the route, vehicle and duration. Send us your details and our team will confirm the price before you book.

What is included in the tour price?

Inclusions depend on the route, vehicle and duration. Our team confirms exactly what is covered before you confirm the booking.
