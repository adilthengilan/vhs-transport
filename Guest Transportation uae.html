Guest transportation, answered.

What is guest transportation?

Guest transportation is a pre-arranged service that moves your visitors, clients or VIP guests between the airport, hotels, offices and sightseeing stops, handled by a professional driver.

Who is guest transportation for?

It suits companies, hotels and organisers hosting visitors, clients, delegations and VIP guests, from a single traveller to a full group.

What guest transport services do you offer?

We offer airport transfers, hotel runs, and half-day and full-day city tours, planned around your guests and their itinerary.

How do I book guest transportation?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp or email info@vhstransport.com and tell us who is arriving, when and where. Our team will arrange the right vehicle and driver.

Do you cover airport arrivals and departures?

Yes. We arrange timely airport pick-ups and drop-offs, with a driver ready on arrival so your guests are never left waiting.

Can you handle groups and delegations?

Yes. Share the itinerary and group size and we will plan pick-ups, drop-offs and sightseeing with the right vehicles, with a coordinator available if plans change.

Which areas do you cover?

We arrange guest transport across Abu Dhabi, including Al Jazira, the city centre, the islands and surrounding districts. For other locations in the UAE, contact our team to confirm.

Is the service available 24 hours?

Yes. VHS Passengers Transport operates 24/7, so early-morning and late-night arrivals can be arranged.

How much does guest transportation cost?

The price depends on the vehicles, routes and duration. Send us the itinerary and our team will confirm the price before the trip.
