FAQ

Oil field transportation, answered.

What is oil field transportation?

Oil field transportation is passenger transport for crews and project teams working on oil and gas sites. VHS Passengers Transport provides it with trained drivers and a 24/7 operations team.

How do I arrange transportation for an oil and gas project?

Call +971 2 440 3060, message us on WhatsApp at +971 56 343 4546 or email info@vhstransport.com with your project, crew size and schedule. Our team builds a transportation plan that fits your operational and safety requirements.

What kind of crew movement do you cover?

We move crews and project teams between accommodation, offices and field locations across Abu Dhabi and the UAE.

How do you approach safety?

Our drivers are trained, and we plan each operation around the site safety requirements you share with us. Send us your requirements and we will confirm how we can meet them.

Can you support changes in shifts and schedules?

Yes. Our operations team works 24/7, so changes in shifts and schedules can be handled at any hour.

Do you provide GPS tracking?

Yes. GPS on our vehicles supports visibility and operational reporting.
